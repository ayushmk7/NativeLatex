Planned workflow

A shorter path from draft to page.

  1. 01

    Open your folder

    Work with ordinary .tex, .md, bibliography, and image files. Your project stays yours.

  2. 02

    Write with room to focus

    Move between source, problems, and preview without juggling separate tools or losing your place.

  3. 03

    Build locally

    Use the TeX tools installed on your Mac. Markdown preview is planned to work without a TeX installation.

Design principles

Your files. Your flow.

Native on Mac
Familiar windows, menus, keyboard paths, and a real PDF preview.
Local by default
Editing and builds are designed to need no account and no internet connection.
More than one project
Keep separate workspaces open without mixing roots or results.
