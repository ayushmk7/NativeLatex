From draft to page.

  1. Open your files

    Edit ordinary .tex, .md, and bibliography files where they already live. Your files stay yours.

  2. Write with room to focus

    Move between source, problems, and preview without juggling separate tools or losing your place.

  3. Build locally

    Build PDFs with the TeX tools installed on your Mac. Markdown previews through Pandoc, if you have it installed.
